Gamma vs Beautiful.ai: Which AI Presentation Maker Is Better?

Published November 2026 · Comparison

Plan details checked against each company's official pricing page as of November 2026. AI presentation pricing changes often, so confirm current prices on the vendor's page before subscribing.

Gamma and Beautiful.ai both turn a prompt into a finished deck, but they work differently. Gamma generates flexible, web-style presentations made of cards and is built around fast first drafts. Beautiful.ai uses Smart Slides, templates that automatically re-arrange themselves as you add content, so decks stay consistent without manual formatting. Gamma's pricing page lists plan features but its dollar amounts did not load when we checked, so confirm Plus and Pro prices at gamma.app/pricing. Beautiful.ai's page describes Team seat counts inconsistently (fixed pricing for three users in one place, per-user pricing for 2 to 20 seats in another), so check how many users your plan includes.

Where each one wins

Gamma wins on speed and a free start. You get 400 AI credits at signup with no credit card, which is enough to generate several full decks and judge the output before paying. The free plan includes basic PPT and PDF export, and paid plans add custom domains and analytics for decks you share as web pages.

Beautiful.ai wins on design consistency. Smart Slides auto-format as content changes, and Pro includes over 300 Smart Slide layouts, custom brand themes, data visualizations and PowerPoint import and export. If your team ships client-facing decks every week and formatting time is the real cost, that automation is the point.

Mind the trial terms. Beautiful.ai's 14-day trial requires a credit card and charges it automatically if you do not cancel in time. Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable, and annual plans are only partially refundable within 24 hours of purchase or renewal. Students with a valid .edu email can get a year free.

Quick take: want to start free and get a draft in minutes? Gamma. Need polished, brand-consistent decks with PowerPoint compatibility and are happy to pay about $14.50/month? Beautiful.ai.

Frequently asked questions

Is Gamma free?
Yes. Gamma has a free plan with 400 AI credits at signup and no credit card required. Free plan decks carry the "Made with Gamma" badge, which paid plans remove.

Does Beautiful.ai have a free plan?
No permanent free plan. Beautiful.ai offers a 14-day free trial of Pro or Team that requires a credit card and converts to a paid plan unless you cancel. Students with a valid .edu email can get a year free.

Which is cheaper, Gamma or Beautiful.ai?
Gamma is the cheaper way to start because it has a real free tier. For paid use, compare the current Gamma Plus price on its pricing page with Beautiful.ai Pro at $14.50/month billed annually.

Can I export to PowerPoint?
Yes on both. Gamma includes basic import and PPT/PDF export on its free plan, and Beautiful.ai supports PowerPoint import and export on its paid plans.

Which is better for teams?
Beautiful.ai is built around team workflows, with real-time collaborative editing, a shared slide and template library and locked brand themes on its Team plan. Gamma suits individuals and small groups who mostly share decks as links.

The bottom line

Pick Gamma if you want a free, fast first draft and web-style sharing, and Beautiful.ai if design consistency and PowerPoint compatibility matter more than price. Because Gamma starts free, the cheapest way to decide is to build the same deck in both and compare the results. If you are also weighing Canva, read our Beautiful.ai vs Canva comparison.
