DALL-E vs Stable Diffusion: What Changed in 2026?

Published November 2026 · Comparison

Checked against OpenAI's deprecations page and Stability AI's license page as of November 2026. Model names and pricing change often, so verify details with the providers before building on either.

If you are searching for DALL-E vs Stable Diffusion, the first thing to know is that the matchup has changed. OpenAI shut down the dall-e-2 and dall-e-3 API endpoints on May 12, 2026, and points developers to its GPT Image models (gpt-image-2, gpt-image-1 and gpt-image-1-mini) instead. So the practical comparison today is OpenAI's closed GPT Image family against Stability AI's open-weight Stable Diffusion models. For the three-way version that includes Midjourney, see Midjourney vs DALL-E vs Stable Diffusion.

Stability AI's Community License covers the Stable Diffusion 3.5 suite for individuals and organizations under $1M in annual revenue, regardless of the source of that revenue. Check OpenAI's pricing page for current per-image API costs.

Where each one wins

GPT Image wins on convenience. There is nothing to install: you describe an image in ChatGPT or call the API and get a result. For beginners and occasional users it is the lowest-friction way to generate images, and it is the supported path now that DALL-E is retired.

Stable Diffusion wins on control and cost at scale. Because the weights are open, you can run it on your own hardware, fine-tune it on your own style or product, and keep the whole pipeline private. Stability states you own the outputs, subject to applicable law and its acceptable use policy. The trade-offs are setup effort and the need for a capable GPU, or a hosted service.

Licensing matters once you grow. Stable Diffusion is free for commercial use only while your organization stays under $1M in annual revenue; above that you need an Enterprise license from Stability AI.

Quick take: want images with no setup? GPT Image in ChatGPT. Want control, privacy and no per-image fees and have the hardware? Stable Diffusion.

Frequently asked questions

Is DALL-E still available?
The DALL-E 2 and DALL-E 3 API endpoints were retired on May 12, 2026. OpenAI recommends gpt-image-2, gpt-image-1 or gpt-image-1-mini as replacements, and image generation in ChatGPT now runs on its GPT Image models.

Is Stable Diffusion free for commercial use?
Yes, under the Stability AI Community License, as long as your organization generates less than $1M in annual revenue. Above that threshold you need an Enterprise license.

Which is better for beginners, DALL-E or Stable Diffusion?
GPT Image through ChatGPT is easier because there is nothing to install. Stable Diffusion rewards people willing to set up software and a GPU, or use a hosted interface, in exchange for more control.

Can I fine-tune Stable Diffusion?
Yes. Stability AI states that LoRAs, fine-tunes and similar derivative works are allowed. The license restricts using the models or their outputs to create a new competing foundation model architecture.

Do I own images made with Stable Diffusion?
According to Stability AI, as between you and Stability you own outputs generated from its Core Models or derivatives, provided you comply with applicable law and its acceptable use policy. This is not legal advice.

The bottom line

DALL-E is gone as a named product, so compare GPT Image with Stable Diffusion instead. Choose GPT Image for simplicity and a supported hosted workflow, and Stable Diffusion when you want ownership of the pipeline, customization and predictable costs, keeping the $1M revenue threshold in mind.
