Best AI Customer Support Tools in 2026: Intercom Fin vs Zendesk AI vs Tidio

Published September 2026 · Comparison

Pricing checked against each provider's official pricing pages and published rate cards as of September 2026. AI support pricing is usage-based and changes often — verify current rates before subscribing.

AI customer support tools have mostly moved to consumption-based pricing — you pay a seat fee for the underlying helpdesk, then a separate per-conversation or per-resolution charge for the AI agent itself. That makes these three genuinely hard to compare on price alone, since your actual bill depends entirely on ticket volume and AI resolution rate. Here's how Intercom Fin, Zendesk AI, and Tidio structure their pricing, and who each one actually fits.

Where each one wins

Intercom Fin is priced simply once you understand the model: a flat $0.99 per resolved conversation regardless of company size, layered on top of your Intercom seat cost. Intercom's own case studies report real-world resolution rates between 42% and 50%, which matters for forecasting — a support team fielding 2,000 tickets a month at a 45% resolution rate would pay roughly $891 in Fin charges alone, on top of seats. Since Salesforce's 2026 acquisition of the renamed "Fin" company, expect continued investment in resolution accuracy.

Zendesk AI makes the most sense for teams already running Zendesk for ticketing, since the AI layer builds on infrastructure you're likely already paying for. The pricing is the most layered of the three — base Suite plan, optional Copilot add-on, and a per-resolution meter — which makes it harder to predict your total bill without a pilot period, but it can work out efficiently at higher volumes where the included per-agent resolutions offset some of the metered cost.

Tidio is built for a completely different buyer: small businesses and online stores that need a chat widget with some AI on top, not an enterprise support platform. Its free plan and $29-59/month tiers are dramatically cheaper than the other two, though the catch is that Lyro AI isn't bundled into cheaper plans — it's a separate add-on, so a fully-loaded setup (Growth plan + Lyro + Flows automation) can land around $200/month rather than the advertised entry price.

Quick take: Intercom Fin for the simplest per-resolution pricing model, Zendesk AI if you're already on Zendesk and have volume to absorb the layered cost, Tidio for small businesses that want AI chat without enterprise-support pricing.

The real lesson: model your own volume

None of these three have a price you can quote without knowing your ticket volume and expected AI resolution rate — that's true by design, since usage-based pricing is meant to scale with your actual support load. Before committing, run a free trial or pilot with each vendor using your real conversation volume, and calculate the fully-loaded monthly cost (seats + AI resolutions + any add-ons) rather than comparing the advertised starting prices.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Intercom Fin AI cost?
$0.99 per resolved outcome, plus your Intercom seat fee, with a 50-outcome monthly minimum.

How much does Zendesk AI cost?
Base Suite plans run $55-$115/agent/month annual, plus roughly $1.50-$2.00 per AI resolution and an optional $50/agent Copilot add-on.

Is there an affordable AI customer support tool for small businesses?
Tidio is the most budget-friendly, with a free tier and paid plans from $29/month, though its Lyro AI is a separate add-on on cheaper tiers.

Which AI support tool is best for a small team on a budget?
Tidio, for accessible self-serve pricing. Intercom Fin and Zendesk AI are built for larger support volumes.

The bottom line

If you're a small business or online store, start with Tidio — its pricing is the most transparent and the entry cost is low enough to test AI support without much risk. If you're a mid-size team that needs strong resolution accuracy and can tolerate per-conversation billing, Intercom Fin has the simplest pricing model of the enterprise-grade options. And if you're already deep in the Zendesk ecosystem, its AI layer is a natural add-on — just budget carefully, since it's the hardest of the three to predict a final bill for.
