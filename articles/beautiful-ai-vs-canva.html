Beautiful.ai vs Canva: Which Is Better for Presentations?

Published November 2026 · Comparison

Beautiful.ai details checked against its official pricing page as of November 2026. We could not load Canva's own pricing page, so Canva figures are approximate and drawn from recent third-party reports; confirm on canva.com/pricing before subscribing.

These two tools solve different problems. Beautiful.ai only makes presentations, and its Smart Slides re-arrange automatically as you add content. Canva is a general design platform where presentations sit alongside social posts, documents and graphics, with AI features such as Magic Design layered on top. Which is better depends on whether decks are your main output or one of many things you design. For a three-way view that adds Gamma, see our presentation makers roundup.

Where each one wins

Beautiful.ai wins when decks are the main job. Smart Slides handle spacing and alignment automatically, Pro includes over 300 Smart Slide layouts, custom brand themes, data visualizations, viewer analytics and PowerPoint import and export. You spend less time formatting and the result stays consistent across a long deck.

Canva wins on breadth and a free start. If you also need social graphics, documents and brand assets, one Canva account covers all of it, and the permanent free plan lets you build presentations without a card. You trade some automatic layout control for flexibility.

Watch the billing details. Beautiful.ai's trial requires a credit card and charges it if you do not cancel within 14 days, and monthly subscriptions are non-refundable. Students with a valid .edu email can get a year of Beautiful.ai free.

Quick take: presentations are most of your work and you want automatic polish? Beautiful.ai. Presentations are one of many design tasks and you want to start free? Canva.

Frequently asked questions

Is Beautiful.ai better than Canva for presentations?
For presentation-only work, usually yes, because Smart Slides auto-format content and keep decks consistent. Canva is better if you also design other content and want one tool for everything.

Is Canva free?
Yes, Canva has a permanent free plan, although AI features such as Magic Design are limited on it. Paid plans unlock higher AI limits and extra features.

Is Beautiful.ai more expensive than Canva?
Beautiful.ai Pro is $14.50/month billed annually. Canva Pro is reported at roughly $10 to $12/month billed annually, so Canva is generally cheaper, and it also has a free plan. Confirm Canva pricing on its site.

Can Beautiful.ai export to PowerPoint?
Yes. Beautiful.ai supports PowerPoint import and export, along with PDF export, on its paid plans.

Is there a free alternative to both?
Gamma offers a free plan with 400 AI credits at signup and no credit card required. See our Gamma vs Beautiful.ai comparison.

The bottom line

If most of your output is slide decks, Beautiful.ai saves formatting time and is worth its price. If presentations are one task among many, or you simply want to start free, Canva is the more flexible choice. Try your real deck in each before committing, since both let you test without paying (Beautiful.ai through its card-required 14-day trial).
